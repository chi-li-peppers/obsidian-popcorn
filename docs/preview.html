Movie Night

A slow evening, one long film, the lights down low.

The room should feel like the screen: warm at the edges, dark in the middle, and quiet enough to hear the projector. This note is the checklist for it.

Before the opening shot

  • Dim the lamps
  • Grain on, letterbox off
  • Queue the intermission note
