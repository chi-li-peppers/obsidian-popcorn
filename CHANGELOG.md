# Changelog

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Released palettes are never renamed or removed.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-23

### Changed

- Distinct film-inspired visual language for every palette: custom typography (serif, monospace, rounded, sans), panel & card corner geometry (0px–26px), tab styles, borders, and subtle textures (Matrix CRT scanlines, Severance Lumon grid, Twin Peaks velvet & chevron, Grand Budapest double frames, Silo blueprint & hazard bar, The Bear ticket borders, Barbie pop-art bubbles)
- Refined glass panels with specular top-edge highlights, tinted active tabs, framed tags, styled callouts, and thin translucent scrollbars
- Regenerated all 22 palette previews, mobile screenshots, contact sheet, palettes GIF, and Obsidian Theme Store thumbnail

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- Glass layout: floating translucent panels on a glowing backdrop, centered pill tabs, floating status bar
- 22 film and TV inspired palettes, each in dark and light
- Per-note palettes through the `cssclasses` property (`pc-<palette>`)
- Style Settings: palette, glass opacity, blur, solid panels, background glow, line width, heading size, classic tabs, ribbon, folder colors, task cards
- Colored folders, task cards, property chips, glass menus and modals
- Graph view and Canvas colors from the active palette
- Styling for Tasks, Dataview, Kanban, Calendar and Excalidraw
- Mobile: floating bottom toolbar, glass drawers, lighter blur, bigger tap targets
- Respects "reduce transparency" in system settings
